Before you write

A few honest answers.

How far in advance should we book?

Six to twelve months for full packages. We do shorter timelines, but the early-bird discount only applies past six months.

Do you do non-Vietnamese weddings?

Many of ours have a non-Vietnamese partner — that's most of who we serve. Pure non-Vietnamese, less often, but yes.

What's the deposit?

25% to lock the date. Refundable up to 90 days out, partially refundable from 90–30, non-refundable after.
